Roadbook

Your ride. Your costs. Your history. Everything in one app.

Roadbook is an Android app for cars and motorcycles. It keeps track of fuel stops, service, expenses and tires for all your vehicles, and keeps a mileage logbook that you can export as a PDF.

What it does

  • Fuel stops and average consumption, costs by category, cost per distance
  • Service reminders, tire garage with age and mounting history
  • Mileage logbook: trips by hand or automatically when your vehicle's paired Bluetooth device connects; business/private; PDF export per vehicle and year
  • Nearby fuel stations with prices (Germany, Austria, France, Spain) and price alerts
  • CSV import and export, foreign currencies, optional backup to your own Google Drive

Your data

Your entries are stored on your device. Read the privacy policy for the details on location, network requests and crash reports.
